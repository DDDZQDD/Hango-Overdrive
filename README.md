## Hango-Overdrive
Hango-Overdrive is a unique 2D roguelike that blends factory management, card creation, and tactical dungeon combat. The player builds and optimizes production lines to craft resources, equipment, and combat cards. When ready, they dive into dungeons where each battle unfolds turn by turn, with no movement—only through combat unit cards, defense cards, and strategic abilities.

Death inside a dungeon is permanent, pushing the player to carefully prepare their production during the factory phase before each raid. Balancing optimization and calculated risk, #0969DA Hango-Overdrive #00000 offers a deep, tense, and highly addictive gameplay loop.
